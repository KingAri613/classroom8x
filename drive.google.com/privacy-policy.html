Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

About Classroom8x

Classroom8x provides access to browser games and related site features. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the site.

Consent and cookies

Classroom8x uses Kookieok to display and manage cookie-consent choices. Analytics and advertising-related consent is denied by default. You can accept, refuse, or change your choices through the consent controls.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site traffic and improve the site. When analytics consent is denied, Google may receive limited cookieless measurement signals if Advanced Consent Mode is enabled. When consent is granted, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies and related identifiers. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms.

Information stored on your device

Classroom8x may use browser local storage to remember settings such as grid layout, keyboard-shortcut preferences, and recently played games. The classroom8x_loader_last_index item remembers the last loading animation shown so the next visit can use a different animation. This is a functional preference, not an analytics or advertising identifier. These settings are stored on your device and are not submitted to Classroom8x as account information.

For details about cookies and local-storage items, see our Cookie Policy.

External game services

Some games are loaded from third-party hosts in an embedded player. Those providers may process technical information, use cookies, or have their own privacy policies. Their practices are outside Classroom8x's control, so please review the applicable provider's policy where appropriate.

Children and schools

The site is intended for use in classroom and school environments. We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information from children. Schools and guardians should make their own decisions about whether the site is appropriate for their users.

Your choices

You can change your consent choice using the Kookieok controls, clear local storage through your browser, or disable cookies in your browser settings. These actions may affect site preferences or analytics functionality.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Classroom8x site administrator through the contact method provided on the site.
