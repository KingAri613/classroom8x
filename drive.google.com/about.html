About Classroom8x

Classroom8x is an independent browser-games website created by a hobby coder. The idea behind the project is simple: make it easier for people to find enjoyable browser games in one place and open them without installing a separate app.

Why Classroom8x was created

Classroom8x grew out of a hobby coding project and an interest in making a useful, easy-to-browse game catalog. The site is independently maintained, and its layout and features are adjusted over time as the project develops.

Finding games

The catalog groups games so visitors can browse or search for a title that interests them. Game availability can vary by device, browser, network, and the service hosting the game. If a game does not load, it may be temporarily unavailable or depend on a third-party provider.

Third-party games and services

Classroom8x is a catalog and access point; it is not the developer or publisher of every game listed here. Some titles are hosted or embedded by third-party services. Those providers control their own games, content, availability, and privacy practices. Game names, artwork, trademarks, and other third-party materials belong to their respective owners. Their appearance on Classroom8x does not imply sponsorship or affiliation.

An independently maintained project

Classroom8x is maintained as a personal hobby project rather than presented as a large software company. The catalog and site features may change, and individual games or links may be added, updated, or removed as the project is maintained. The site aims to make browsing straightforward while being clear that external games remain under their providers' control.
