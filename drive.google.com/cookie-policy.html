How Classroom8x uses cookies

Classroom8x uses Kookieok to manage your consent choices. Analytics and advertising-related storage is denied by default and is used only according to your consent selection.

NameTypePurposeDuration

This item is a functional preference. It is not used to identify you, measure advertising, or track your activity across websites. It is not a cookie and cannot be controlled by changing cookie settings, although you can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Analytics and third-party services

Google Analytics 4 may use analytics storage when you grant analytics consent. When consent is denied, Advanced Consent Mode may send limited cookieless measurement signals. Embedded games and other external services may set their own cookies or use other storage under their own policies.

Managing your choices

You can accept, refuse, or update consent through the Kookieok banner. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings.
